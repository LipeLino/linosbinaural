# Listening guide evidence and wording

This module is a guide to choosing a *sound*, not a prediction of a listener's EEG or a treatment. `BAND_GUIDES` follows the application's selected beat boundaries in `mix.ts` (0.5–<4, 4–<8, 8–<13, 13–<30, 30–40 Hz). EEG band edges vary among research groups; these exact cutoffs are a product labeling convention, not a clinical standard. `association` pairs a common listening intention with a **separate EEG observation**. `evidence` states why the audio difference cannot be assumed to cause the associated state. The app does not measure EEG.

- [Ingendoh, Posny & Heine, PLOS ONE (2023)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286023) reviewed 14 eligible EEG studies of binaural beats. Five supported entrainment, eight contradicted it, and one was mixed; methods and measures varied. Their discussion does not identify a dependable beat frequency for sleep, calm, focus, or cognition. The paper also describes a binaural percept mainly around 1–30 Hz and notes that, above about 30 Hz, some listeners hear separate tones; this threshold varies by person and setup. That is why the 30–40 Hz option is explicitly experimental rather than a guaranteed gamma beat.
- [Corona-González et al., Frontiers in Psychology (2021)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.764068/full) describes the common delta/deep-sleep, theta/drowsiness, alpha/rest, beta/active-thinking, and gamma/cognitive-process EEG associations. In its own 20-person theta-versus-beta binaural experiment, the expected distinct mental states were **not** established. The guide uses the associations to explain familiar band names, not to claim the sounds reproduce them.
- [Marcantoni et al., Sensors (2023)](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/23/13/5968) reviewed EEG indices of mental involvement and notes that band thresholds and interpretations are not standardized enough to select a universal best index for a specific use. This supports describing the band's boundaries as labels rather than precise biological targets.

`PRESET_GUIDES` covers exactly the five `FACTORY_PRESETS` IDs. Moon/shore/café/lantern copy describes a possible listening context without an outcome promise. Forest explicitly has `binaural.enabled === false`: it is ambience-only, so no binaural frequency or headphone claim belongs to that preset. None of the preset durations is an evidence-based treatment dose. Binaural listening uses separated stereo channels; ambience-only listening does not require headphones. These statements are consistent with the existing evidence page and product specification.
